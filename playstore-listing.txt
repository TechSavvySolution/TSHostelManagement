🏠 TS Hostel Management — Your All-in-One Smart Hostel Companion

Managing a hostel has never been this seamless. TS Hostel Management is a feature-rich, role-based Android application designed for educational institutions to digitize and streamline every aspect of hostel operations — from room assignments and fee collection to complaint tracking and daily announcements.

─── WHO IS IT FOR? ───────────────────────────

👨‍🎓 HOSTELERS
Stay on top of everything hostel-related from the palm of your hand:
• View your room, floor, and block assignment at a glance.
• Meet your roommates — see all co-residents in your room with contact details.
• Raise complaints with photos or video attachments, and track their resolution status in real time.
• Chat directly with the staff member handling your complaint.
• Check your hostel fee status, pay via UPI in one tap, and keep payment records.
• Stay informed with the latest announcements and the daily mess menu.
• Customize your profile with a photo and personal details.

👷 HOSTEL STAFF
Efficiently resolve and manage resident issues:
• View all assigned complaints with subject, description, and media attachments.
• Update complaint status (Pending → In Progress → Resolved).
• Chat with hostelers to clarify and communicate updates.

🔧 ADMINISTRATORS
Complete control over the entire hostel ecosystem:
• Manage hostel infrastructure — add, edit, or remove Blocks, Floors, and Rooms.
• View room occupancy, student assignments, and capacity stats per room/floor/block.
• Add and manage all hosteler and staff user accounts.
• Assign hostelers to specific rooms with a simple form.
• Publish semester fee records to all hostelers in one click.
• Mark fees as Paid (manual) or verify UPI payments by transaction ID.
• Create, edit, and pin announcements for residents.
• Manage the daily and weekly mess menu.
• View and manage all complaints across the hostel.

─── KEY FEATURES ─────────────────────────────

✅ Role-based access — Hosteler, Staff, and Admin dashboards
✅ Real-time data sync powered by Firebase Firestore
✅ Complaint system with image & video attachment support
✅ In-app chat between hostelers and staff per complaint
✅ UPI fee payment with one-tap redirect & transaction tracking
✅ CDN-powered profile photos & complaint media uploads
✅ Roommate viewer — see who shares your room
✅ Infrastructure management — Blocks, Floors, Rooms
✅ Announcements with rich content for all residents
✅ Mess menu management by administrators
✅ Soft-delete user management (preserves history)
✅ Premium dark-mode UI with glassmorphism design

─── SECURITY & PRIVACY ───────────────────────

🔒 Secured with Firebase Authentication
🔒 Role-based Firestore security rules
🔒 UPI payments handled entirely by your UPI app — no bank data stored
🔒 CDN uploads protected with API token authentication
